Our Events

We host monthly pop-up shops across the city. Here's what to expect:

What We Do

  • Clothing Distribution: We set up racks with donated clothes. Guests can browse and choose what they like.
  • Styling Assistance: Volunteers help guests find outfits that suit them.
  • Warm Meals: We provide food and drinks to all guests.
  • Referral Services: We connect guests with shelters and social services.
