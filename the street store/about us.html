About The Street Store

Our History

The Street Store was founded in 2015 by social workers and community activists in Johannesburg. They noticed that traditional clothing handouts often made people experiencing homelessness feel embarrassed and undervalued. Instead of simply distributing clothes in bags, they created a "pop-up shop" on the city streets where homeless people could browse, try on, and choose clothes with dignity.

Our Mission

To restore dignity to people experiencing homelessness by providing a compassionate, retail-style clothing distribution experience where guests can choose items that reflect their personal style and needs.

Our Vision

A world where everyone has access to warm, appropriate clothing and is treated with dignity and respect, regardless of their circumstances.
